About Us

The ISBAT University Student ICT Training Centre helps students learn practical computer skills that are needed in the job market today.

Our trainers guide students through hands-on lessons in web development, networking, databases and cybersecurity.

How to Register

  1. The student visits the university website or IT training registration portal and selects the IT Training Program.
  2. The student creates an account by providing: Full name, Student registration number (if applicable), Email address, Phone number and Password.
  3. The student logs in using the registered email address or student number and password.
  4. The system displays the four available courses. The student selects one or more courses depending on their interests and the university's registration requirements.
